Slope trainer
guess first, then check · teach the eye
Not zeroed. Your phone's camera bump tilts ~1°+ — that's 2%+ of fake slope baked into every reading. Calibrate once on a flat surface.
Read the slope with your eye. Lock in your guess. Then lay the phone flat on the green along the fall line and reveal the truth. Over time your guesses close in on the level — and you won't need the phone on the course.
1
Find a surface you know is dead flat — a kitchen counter, glass table, granite/quartz countertop. Avoid tablecloths, wood that may warp, anything spongy.
2
Lay your phone face-up on the surface, with case on (or off — match how you'll use it on the green).
3
Wait for the reading to settle, then tap Capture zero. Whatever it shows now becomes "flat" — every future reading subtracts this offset.
